Tourist Guides

Find a guide, keep their records up to date, and answer what they send you.

helpHow tourist guides work expand_more

  1. Step 1 · You

    Add the guide

    Press New guide record. Choose whether they cover the whole municipality or one barangay. A barangay guide can only be given destinations in that barangay.

  2. Step 2 · You

    Give them a sign-in

    Optional. With a sign-in, the guide keeps their own availability, working days and languages up to date in the guide portal.

  3. Step 3 · You

    Assign their tours

    Done on Guide Bookings. ZTIMS refuses a guide who is unavailable, not working that day, or already booked at that time.

  4. Step 4 · The guide

    Sends you reports

    Tour reports arrive under Reports. If a guide's phone number or "About" changes, they tell you and you update it with Edit.
