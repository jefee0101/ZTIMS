Guide Bookings

Visitors book a guide on the website, without an account, and pay here at the office. Each tab below is one step. Work from left to right.

helpHow guide bookings work expand_more

  1. Step 1 · The visitor

    Books on the website

    They choose a destination, date and time, and get a reference such as TG-2026-00001. Nothing for you to do yet.

  2. Step 2 · You

    Collect the payment

    When they come to the office, find their reference under Collect payment, take the fee and record it. That confirms the booking.

  3. Step 3 · You

    Assign a guide

    Under Assign a guide, pick a guide who serves that destination. ZTIMS refuses a guide who is off or already booked at that time.

  4. Step 4 · After the tour

    Close the booking

    Under Upcoming tours, mark it Completed, or No show if the visitor did not come. Nothing is ever deleted.
