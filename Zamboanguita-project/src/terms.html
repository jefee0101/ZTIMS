1. About this site

The Zamboanguita Tourism Information Management System (ZTIMS, "the site", "we") is run by the Municipal Tourism Office of Zamboanguita, Negros Oriental, Philippines. It publishes the municipality's tourist destinations and enterprises, sells entrance tickets to the attractions it runs, and gives directions to each place.

By using the site you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use it.

2. Using ZTIMS

  • The site is free to use and needs no account. Visitors are never asked to register or sign in.
  • Use it for planning and enjoying a visit to Zamboanguita. Please do not copy the catalogue in bulk, flood the site with automated requests, or try to reach the staff-only areas.
  • The staff sign-in is for Tourism Office personnel and the managers of listed enterprises only. Attempting to use someone else's account is not permitted.

3. Listings and their accuracy

Each listing is written by the enterprise that runs it or by the Tourism Office, and the Office reviews what is published. Even so, entrance fees, opening hours, contact numbers and amenities change, and a listing may lag behind. Confirm the details that matter to you with the enterprise before you travel.

  • ZTIMS does not own or operate the resorts, dive shops or other enterprises it lists. Your stay, meal or activity is between you and them.
  • The Tourism Office may hide a listing at any time — for example while it is being verified. Listings are never deleted, so a place that reappears is the same record, not a new one.
  • If a listing is wrong, tell us through the feedback form and we will check it.

4. Entrance tickets

  • Some attractions the Tourism Office runs sell entrance tickets online. A ticket is for one attraction on one date, for the number of people of each kind on it, and gets them in once.
  • Everyone pays the regular price, except students and children where the Office sets a price for them. A student's school ID is checked at the entrance.
  • Tickets are not sold for days the attraction is closed.
  • Online payment happens on the payment service's own page and currently runs in test mode (a demonstration; no real money moves). ZTIMS itself never asks for card or e-wallet details: anyone asking for them in ZTIMS's name, by message, call or any page that is not the payment service's own, is not us.

Moving or cancelling

  • You can move the date, or cancel, on the Manage my ticket page with your code and email address, until 11:59 PM (Philippine time) on the day before your visit. After that it can no longer be changed.
  • When you cancel, you get back the amount paid less the share the Tourism Office keeps for that destination, which is shown before you confirm. When the Office cancels or closes the attraction, you get everything back, or may move to another date.
  • A ticket that has been used at the entrance is never refunded.
  • Refunds go back the way you paid. In test mode no real money moves.

5. Maps and directions

The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data and routes are calculated by third-party routing services (see the Privacy Policy for which). They are offered as guidance only.

  • Road, sea and weather conditions change. Check them yourself, especially before a boat crossing or a trip into the upland barangays.
  • Estimated times and distances are the routing provider's figures and are not guaranteed.
  • You are responsible for your own safety while travelling. Follow local advice and signage over the map.

6. Feedback you send

The Contact Us page lets you send feedback about the site or a listing. By sending it you allow the Tourism Office to read it, act on it and keep it as a record. Please do not include passwords, card numbers or anyone else's personal details, and keep it civil — abusive messages are not acted on.

7. Enterprise accounts

The Tourism Office issues accounts to the managers of listed enterprises so they can keep their own listing current. If you hold one:

  • You are responsible for what you publish being accurate and yours to publish, including photos.
  • Keep your password private. Anything done under your account is treated as done by you.
  • The Tourism Office may hide a listing, or suspend an account, where these terms or local regulations are not met. Nothing is deleted; a listing can be restored once the issue is resolved.

8. Photos and content

Text and photographs on the site belong to the Municipal Tourism Office or to the enterprise that supplied them. You may share links to any page freely. Reusing the photographs or the catalogue elsewhere needs the owner's permission. Map tiles are © OpenStreetMap contributors.

9. Our responsibility

We take reasonable care to keep the site accurate and available, but it is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, the Municipal Tourism Office is not liable for loss arising from reliance on a listing, a route, an estimated travel time, or from the site being unavailable. The first load of the day can be slow while the server wakes up; this is normal and nothing is lost.

10. Changes and governing law

We may update these terms; the date at the top tells you when. Continued use after a change means you accept it. These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

Questions about these terms go to the Municipal Tourism Office through the Contact Us page, or in person at the Municipal Hall, Zamboanguita.
