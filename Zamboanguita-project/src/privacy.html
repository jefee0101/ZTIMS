1. Who we are

The Zamboanguita Tourism Information Management System (ZTIMS) is operated by the Municipal Tourism Office of Zamboanguita, Negros Oriental, Philippines, which is the personal information controller for anything described here. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

2. What we collect and why

Browsing the site collects nothing about you. Personal information reaches us only when you choose to send it: when you create an account (see Your account), and when you use it as below.

When you buy an entrance ticket

  • The name the tickets are under and your email — they come from your account; the email is where we send your ticket and receipt. Only your account can open the ticket to move or cancel it.
  • The date of your visit and how many people of each kind (regular, student, child) — to price the tickets and to tell the entrance staff how many people to let in.
  • We do not collect or keep a copy of any ID. A student's school ID is only looked at by staff at the entrance, to check the student price, and handed straight back.

When you request a room

  • Your name, email, phone number and the country where you live — so the resort can answer you and reach you, and so its Guest Register can count guests by country of residence, as the provincial tourism report asks. Only your account can open the booking to move or cancel it.
  • Your dates, how many adults and children, the rooms you chose, and any note you write — to hold the rooms and work out the price.
  • No payment details: you pay the resort directly, outside ZTIMS.

When you pay online

  • Online payment is offered as a demonstration in the payment service's test mode: no real money moves. You can always pay onsite at the Municipal Tourism Office instead.
  • You pay on Xendit's own page. Your card number, wallet PIN and bank details go to Xendit, never to ZTIMS. We do not send Xendit your name, email or phone number.
  • ZTIMS keeps only what a receipt needs: the amount, the method (for example GCash), Xendit's payment reference, and any refund.

Emails we send you

  • To the email you gave, and only about your own ticket: the receipt, and notices when it is moved, cancelled or refunded, or when the office has to close an attraction on your date.
  • For a room request: that it was received, and when the resort confirms (with how to pay it), declines, or does not answer in time, or when the dates change or it is cancelled.
  • No newsletters and no marketing, ever.

When you save a place or write a review

  • Saved places are a list of the places you saved, kept with your account so you find them again. Only you see it.
  • A review keeps your rating (1 to 5 stars), your text, the date, and which ticket or stay showed you had been there. Reviews are public: other visitors see them under your first name and the first letter of your last name (for example "Maria S."), never your email.
  • You can edit or delete your own review at any time. The Tourism Office may hide a review, giving a reason, for example one that names a private person or gives a phone number; a hidden review is no longer public.
  • Enterprise managers see reviews only as the public does, and cannot reply.

When you send feedback

  • Your message and the topic you chose.
  • Your name and email, only if you give them. Both are optional; leave them blank and the message is still read, we just cannot reply.
  • The ZTIMS page you came from, when your browser passes it on, so the officer knows which listing or page you mean. Only pages on this site are noted.

When you ask for directions from where you are

  • If you press the button to route from your current location and your browser allows it, your coordinates are sent to our server once, used to calculate that route, and discarded. They are never written to the database, never attached to a booking, and never used to work out where you have been.
  • You can always type a starting point instead, and the site works without location permission.

Technical information

  • Like any website, our server sees the IP address each request comes from. It is used to limit how fast one connection can buy tickets, send feedback and request routes, and appears in the hosting provider's ordinary server logs. We do not use it to identify or profile you.
  • There are no analytics or advertising cookies and no third-party tracking scripts.

3. Your account

You need an account to buy entrance tickets, request rooms, save places and write reviews. Browsing, the map and directions need none.

  • What an account keeps: your name, your email, a scrambled form of your password (never the password itself), and, once you give them, your phone number and the country where you live. Your phone number and country are asked only when a ticket or room needs them.
  • Confirming your email: we email you a link once, to check the address is yours. Links in our emails carry a one-time code, never your email or password.
  • Signing in with Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your name, your email (only one Google has confirmed) and a number that identifies your Google account to ZTIMS. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or anything else, and we do not post to or read from your Google account. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google records about the sign-in.
  • Tickets and bookings made before accounts with the same email join your account when you first sign in with that confirmed email.
  • How long it stays: until you delete it. It is not erased for being unused.
  • Deleting your account (My account → Security → Delete account, with your password or a fresh Google sign-in): your name, email, phone number, country and password are erased at once, every sign-in ends, and your saved places are removed. Your tickets and bookings keep their own details until the one-year rule below, as municipal and resort records. Your reviews stay, shown as "Former visitor", so places' ratings stay honest; delete a review first if you want it gone. The same email can sign up again later, as a new account.

4. What stays in your browser

The site remembers a little on your own device, in your browser's local storage, and none of it is sent to us:

  • Your theme choice (dark or light), so the next page opens the way you left it.
  • When you are signed in, a sign-in token and your name, so the site knows it is you without asking again for 30 days. Signing out removes them. Staff portals keep their own token the same way.
  • While you sign in, the page you were on, so you come back to it.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.

5. Services that see requests

ZTIMS relies on a few outside services to work. Your browser or our server talks to them directly, so they see the requests involved. Apart from the hosts that store our records and the email service that delivers your own emails, none of them is sent your name, email or ticket details; the most a map service receives is the coordinates of a route you asked for.

  • OpenStreetMap — the map images. Your browser fetches the tiles for the area you are looking at.
  • OpenRouteService, OSRM and Valhalla — calculate routes. Our server sends them the start and end coordinates of a directions request and nothing else. Which one is used depends on the travel mode.
  • OpenRouteService and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — turn a typed place name into coordinates for the address search, and the other way round when a manager pins a location.
  • Cloudinary — hosts the listing photographs. Your browser loads them from there.
  • Google Fonts, Tailwind CSS and unpkg — deliver the site's typefaces, styles and map library. Your browser fetches these files from them.
  • Xendit — the payment page, when you choose to pay online. It sees what you pay with; ZTIMS does not.
  • Google (Gmail) — delivers the Tourism Office's emails to you, so it handles your email address and the message.
  • Google sign-in — only if you choose "Continue with Google": the sign-in and account pages load Google's sign-in button, and Google tells us your name and confirmed email.
  • Vercel (this site and our server) and Supabase (our database, in Singapore) — host the system. Accounts, tickets, bookings, payments, reviews and feedback are stored in the database.

Each of these has its own privacy policy that covers what it logs on its own side.

6. Who we share with

  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for marketing.
  • Reviews are public, under your first name and last initial, as written in What we collect.
  • A room request goes to that resort: its Enterprise Manager sees your name, email, phone, country, dates, guests and note, to answer and host you. The resort keeps its own records under its own obligations.
  • Tourism reporting to provincial and national bodies uses counts only, never individual records.
  • We will disclose information where the law requires it, and only to the extent required.

7. How long we keep it

Only as long as it is needed, as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 requires.

  • Your account stays until you delete it; deleting erases your details at once (see Your account). Your saved places go with it.
  • Reviews stay until you delete them, or, after your account is deleted, as "Former visitor".
  • Your name, phone number and email on a ticket are erased automatically one year after the date of your visit. That year covers the visit itself, any refund or complaint about it, and the year's tourism reports.
  • What stays after that names nobody: the date, the destination, how many people came, and the amount paid with its official receipt number. The municipality keeps these to count visitors and to account for money collected and refunded.
  • A room booking loses your name, email, phone and note one year after check-out; the dates, rooms, number of guests, country and price stay, naming nobody.
  • Feedback loses your name and email one year after it has been resolved; the message itself is kept as a record of what was reported and fixed.
  • Your location for a directions request is not kept at all.
  • You may ask for your details to be erased sooner (see Your rights).

8. How it is protected

  • Everything between your browser, this site and our server travels over HTTPS.
  • Tickets, payments and feedback can only be read by signed-in Tourism Office staff. Enterprise managers see only their own listings and the room requests made to them, never tickets or feedback. Support Staff of the Tourism Office, each looking after one attraction the office runs, see only that attraction's details and its visitor counts (numbers by country, never names), never tickets, payments, bookings or feedback. A visitor's own tickets and bookings open only for the visitor's signed-in account; there is no search by code or email.
  • Passwords are stored only scrambled (bcrypt), must be at least 10 characters and not a common password, and are never sent in emails. Changing your password, or "Sign out of all devices", ends every other sign-in at once. A Google sign-in is checked by our server with Google before it is accepted.
  • A visitor's sign-in never opens a staff portal, and a staff sign-in never opens a visitor's account.
  • Only you can change or delete your reviews; only the Tourism Office can hide one, and it must give a reason.
  • Public forms and sign-in are rate-limited to blunt abuse. Secret keys for outside services are kept on the server and never sent to the browser.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your information has been exposed, tell us through the Contact Us page and we will investigate.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may:

  • Ask what we hold about you and get a copy. Most of it is in My account: your profile, tickets, bookings, saved places and reviews.
  • Have it corrected if it is wrong — your name and phone in My account → Profile, or ask us for a misspelled name on a ticket.
  • Delete your account yourself, at any time, in My account → Security.
  • Ask for it to be erased or blocked where the law allows; note that a used ticket and its payment are a municipal record and may need to be kept.
  • Object to a use you were not told about here, and complain to the National Privacy Commission if you are not satisfied with our answer.

To exercise any of these, contact the Municipal Tourism Office through the Contact Us page or in person at the Municipal Hall. Quote your ticket or booking code if you have one; it lets us find the record without asking for anything more.

10. Children

The site is written for the general public. Accounts are for adults: a ticket or booking should be made by an adult in the group, who is the person named on it. A ticket may count children in the group for the child's price, but no child's name or age is recorded.
