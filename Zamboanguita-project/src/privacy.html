Privacy Policy

ZTIMS is built to collect as little as possible. There are no visitor accounts, no advertising trackers and no online payments. This page lists exactly what the site does handle, why, and who else sees it.

1. Who we are

The Zamboanguita Tourism Information Management System (ZTIMS) is operated by the Municipal Tourism Office of Zamboanguita, Negros Oriental, Philippines, which is the personal information controller for anything described here. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

2. What we collect and why

Browsing the site collects nothing about you. Personal information reaches us only when you choose to send it:

When you request a tourist guide

  • Your name, contact number and email — so the Tourism Office can recognise you at the counter and reach you about the booking.
  • Your nationality — the Office reports the number of domestic and foreign arrivals to provincial and national tourism bodies. Only the count is reported, never who you are.
  • Group size, preferred date and time, and any notes you add — to plan the tour and assign a guide.

When you send feedback

  • Your message and the topic you chose.
  • Your name and email, only if you give them. Both are optional; leave them blank and the message is still read, we just cannot reply.
  • The ZTIMS page you came from, when your browser passes it on, so the officer knows which listing or page you mean. Only pages on this site are noted.

When you ask for directions from where you are

  • If you press the button to route from your current location and your browser allows it, your coordinates are sent to our server once, used to calculate that route, and discarded. They are never written to the database, never attached to a booking, and never used to work out where you have been.
  • You can always type a starting point instead, and the site works without location permission.

Technical information

  • Like any website, our server sees the IP address each request comes from. It is used to limit how fast one connection can submit bookings, feedback and route requests, and appears in the hosting provider's ordinary server logs. We do not use it to identify or profile you.
  • There are no analytics or advertising cookies and no third-party tracking scripts.

3. What stays in your browser

The site remembers a little on your own device, in your browser's local storage, and none of it is sent to us:

  • Your theme choice (dark or light), so the next page opens the way you left it.
  • For Tourism Office staff and establishment managers only, the sign-in token that keeps them signed in to their portal. Visitors never have one.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.

4. Services that see requests

ZTIMS relies on a few outside services to work. Your browser or our server talks to them directly, so they see the requests involved. We never send them your name, email or booking details; the most any of them receives is the coordinates of a route you asked for.

  • OpenStreetMap — the map images. Your browser fetches the tiles for the area you are looking at.
  • OpenRouteService, OSRM and Valhalla — calculate routes. Our server sends them the start and end coordinates of a directions request and nothing else. Which one is used depends on the travel mode.
  • OpenRouteService and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — turn a typed place name into coordinates for the address search, and the other way round when a manager pins a location.
  • Cloudinary — hosts the listing photographs. Your browser loads them from there.
  • Google Fonts, Tailwind CSS and unpkg — deliver the site's typefaces, styles and map library. Your browser fetches these files from them.
  • Vercel (this site), Render (our server) and MongoDB Atlas (our database) — host the system. Bookings and feedback are stored in the database.

Each of these has its own privacy policy that covers what it logs on its own side.

5. Who we share with

  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for marketing.
  • Your assigned tourist guide is told the name the booking is under, the group size, and the date and time — what they need to meet you.
  • Tourism reporting to provincial and national bodies uses counts only (for example, how many domestic and foreign visitors booked in a month), never individual records.
  • We will disclose information where the law requires it, and only to the extent required.

6. How long we keep it

  • Guide bookings are municipal tourism records and are kept as such. A cancelled booking is marked cancelled, not erased, so the record of what happened stays complete.
  • Feedback is kept while it is being acted on and afterwards as a record of what was reported and fixed.
  • Your location for a directions request is not kept at all.

7. How it is protected

  • Everything between your browser, this site and our server travels over HTTPS.
  • Bookings and feedback can only be read by signed-in Tourism Office staff. Establishment managers see only their own listings, never bookings or feedback.
  • Public forms are rate-limited to blunt abuse. Secret keys for outside services are kept on the server and never sent to the browser.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your information has been exposed, tell us through the Contact Us page and we will investigate.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may:

  • Ask what we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Have it corrected if it is wrong — for example, a misspelled name on a booking.
  • Ask for it to be erased or blocked where the law allows; note that a completed booking is a municipal record and may need to be kept.
  • Object to a use you were not told about here, and complain to the National Privacy Commission if you are not satisfied with our answer.

To exercise any of these, contact the Municipal Tourism Office through the Contact Us page or in person at the Municipal Hall. Quote your booking reference if you have one; it lets us find the record without asking for anything more.

9. Children

The site is written for the general public and asks for no age. A guide booking should be made by an adult in the group, who is the person named on it.

10. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. Significant changes will be noted on this page for a period afterwards.
