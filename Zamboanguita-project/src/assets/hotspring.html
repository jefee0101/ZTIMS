Hidden Gem of Zamboanguita

Jomao-as Hotspring

Jomao-as Hot Spring in Malongcay Diot, Zamboanguita, Negros Oriental, is a tranquil, natural thermal spring known for its therapeutic, mineral-rich warm waters and lush surroundings. Located about 30-40 minutes from Dumaguete City, this affordable, rustic getaway offers a "2-in-1" experience with nearby natural, cooler water pools alongside the hot spring
