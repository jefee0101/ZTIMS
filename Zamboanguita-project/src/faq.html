Visiting & browsing

Do I need an account to use ZTIMS?

No. Visitors never sign up or sign in. Browsing, directions and guide bookings all work without one. The sign-in link in the footer is for Tourism Office staff and the managers of listed establishments.

Is the site free?

Yes. ZTIMS is a service of the Municipal Tourism Office. The only fee anywhere in the system is a tourist guide's fee, and that is paid in cash at the office, not on the site.

Are the fees and opening hours on a listing up to date?

Each listing is maintained by the establishment itself or by the Tourism Office, and we keep them as current as we can — but prices and hours do change. For anything that would spoil your day if it were wrong, confirm with the establishment before you go. If you find a listing that is out of date, tell us and we will check it.

How do I find a specific kind of place, like a dive site or a waterfall?

On the Destinations page, use the category chips to narrow the grid, or type into the search box — it matches names, descriptions and barangays as you type.

Where is Zamboanguita?

On the south-eastern coast of Negros Oriental, about 28 kilometres south of Dumaguete City. The Our History page tells the story of the town and its name.

Tourist guides & booking

How do I book a tourist guide?

Open the destination's page. If it requires a guide, you will see the guide fee and a booking form: your name, contact number, email, nationality, group size, and the date and time you would like. Submit it and you get a reference number such as TG-2026-00012.

Then come to the Municipal Tourism Office, quote the reference, and pay the fee at the counter. The officer records the payment, confirms the booking and assigns your guide.

Can I pay online?

No, and we never ask for card or e-wallet details. Payment is in person at the Municipal Tourism Office. A booking is not confirmed until the officer has recorded your payment.

Is my booking confirmed as soon as I submit it?

Not yet — it is a request. It becomes confirmed when you pay at the office. Until then no guide is held for you, so try to come by before your preferred date.

How do I check my booking?

Quote your reference number on the destination page's booking section, or at the office. The site shows only the destination, date, time, group size and status against a reference — never the name or contact details on it — so a reference alone cannot expose anyone.

Why does the site need my nationality?

The Tourism Office reports how many domestic and foreign visitors it served to the provincial and national tourism offices. Only the count is reported; your name is never attached to it. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I choose my guide?

The Tourism Office assigns guides, matching availability and group size. If you have a preference, put it in the notes and the officer will do their best.

What if I have to cancel or I miss the tour?

Let the office know as early as you can, quoting your reference. The booking is marked cancelled (or no-show) rather than deleted, so the record stays clear. Ask at the counter about the refund of a fee already paid.

Map & directions

How do I get directions to a destination?

Every destination page has a Directions panel. Route from your current location, or type a starting point, pick a travel mode, and the route is drawn on the map with its distance and estimated time.

Does the site track my location?

No. If you allow it, your position is sent once to calculate the route you asked for and then discarded. It is never stored and never linked to a booking. You can always type a starting point instead.

Why is a travel mode missing — motorbike, or bicycle?

The site only offers the modes its routing services can currently calculate. If a provider is unavailable, its mode is left out rather than shown and then failing. Car is always available.

How accurate are the routes and travel times?

They are calculated from OpenStreetMap road data by outside routing services and are guidance, not a promise. Times assume normal conditions. Check road, sea and weather conditions yourself, especially for boat crossings and the upland barangays.

For establishments

How do I get my resort, dive shop or restaurant listed?

Ask at the Municipal Tourism Office. The office creates a Tourist Establishment Manager account for you, and you sign in to add and keep up your own listing — description, photos, fees, hours, contact details and the pin on the map. What you publish goes live straight away; the office can unpublish a listing while it checks something.

I manage a listing and forgot my password.

The Tourism Office can set a new password on your account — ask at the counter, or contact us from the email address on the account. You will be given a temporary password to change once you are in.

Why is my listing not showing on the public site?

Either it is unpublished — by you, or by the Tourism Office while something is checked — or the establishment account is marked inactive. Your portal shows the status of each listing. If you cannot see why, contact us.

About this site

Why is the first page load slow sometimes?

The server goes to sleep when nobody has used it for a while, and waking it can take up to a minute. The page tells you when this is happening. Once it is awake, everything is quick again — nothing is broken and nothing you typed is lost.

How do I switch between dark and light mode?

Use the sun/moon button at the top right. The choice is remembered on your device.

I found a mistake, or something is broken. Where do I report it?

On the Contact Us page. Pick the topic that fits — a wrong listing, a problem with the site, a suggestion — and describe it. Leave your email if you would like a reply.

Who runs ZTIMS?

The Municipal Tourism Office of Zamboanguita, Negros Oriental. See the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy for how the site is run and what it keeps.
