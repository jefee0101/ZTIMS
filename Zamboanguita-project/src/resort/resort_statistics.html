Monthly Report

Your guests for the month, counted by where they live, for the Municipal Tourism Office's Form A4. Send it by the 5th of the following month. Counts only: no revenue, and no guest's name or details.

What to count
  • Guests: each guest who checked in this month, once per stay, under the country where they live. Split them into male and female.
  • Number of rooms: the rooms you have for guests.
  • Room-nights occupied: each night a room was taken counts once. 1 room for 3 nights = 3.
  • Guest nights: every guest × every night. A family of 4 for 3 nights = 12.
  • A guest whose residence you don't know goes under "Unspecified residence".
