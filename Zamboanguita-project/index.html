Zamboanguita is a coastal municipality in Negros Oriental, Philippines, located at approximately 9°6′N latitude and 123°12′E longitude, about 28 kilometers south of Dumaguete City.

In Zamboanguita, we prioritize high-end experiences that leave no footprint, only memories. We partner with local guardians to ensure that the beauty we share today remains untainted for the narratives of tomorrow.

"We don't inherit the land from our ancestors, we borrow it from our children."
