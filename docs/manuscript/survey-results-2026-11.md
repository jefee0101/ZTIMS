# Survey results, November 2026 (132 end users)

Computed on 16 November 2026 from the two tally sheets the user shared (Google Sheets: "PREDEV SURVEY TALLY" and "TAM 132 Tourist/Enterprises/Tourism officer"). These are the figures now in the manuscript's Chapter III Tables 5, 6 and 9. Respondents: 100 tourists, 7 Tourism Officers and 25 Enterprise Managers (the user's figures; the sheets have no column for the respondent type).

## Method

- Each item: WM = mean of its valid ratings; SD = sample standard deviation; interpretation from the five-point bands (4.21–5.00, 3.41–4.20, 2.61–3.40, 1.81–2.60, 1.00–1.80).
- Each area or construct: every respondent's average of their valid ratings in it, then the mean and sample SD of those averages (the method stated in Chapter II).
- A blank or double-marked item is left out of that item only.

## Checks on the tallies

- Both sheets have 132 rows (respondents 1–132). Every respondent answered every area of the pre-development survey, although Appendix B had said areas were for certain respondent types only; by the user's choice (16 Nov) the manuscript now says all 132 answered all statements, and the "(Answerable by … only)" lines were removed from Appendix B.
- Pre-development, blank: Part II items 1 (respondent 41), 2 (116), 4 (76), 5 (7), 10 (120, 127), 11 (15), 13 (121), 14 (86), 15 (129); Part III items 3 (9), 9 (129), 13 (21). Double-marked: Part II item 3 (respondent 90), item 9 (120); Part III item 3 (10).
- TAM, double-marked: PU4 (respondents 129, 130, 131), PEOU4 (118). Blank: PEOU4 (110), ATU3 (76), ATU4 (67, 68), BI1 (62–75, fourteen in a row; check the paper forms).
- The sheet's Part III columns are headed N, R, S, O, A (the frequency scale); they were read as 1–5 on the agreement scale of Appendix B.
