Decisions this needs from you

Whose money?Today the payment account is the municipality's demonstration account. Paying real resorts online would need each resort's own merchant account (or a split-payment setup).
When does the visitor pay?Shown here: after the resort confirms. The other way is at request, with an automatic refund if declined.
Full amount or a deposit?Shown here: full. A deposit would be a second option.
Counts.A payment is never a visitor count; Statistics stay counts only.
